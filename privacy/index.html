Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

On your device

SnapHSK provides offline HSK 2.0 vocabulary practice. You do not need an account. Vocabulary, learning progress, saved words, recent-word history, and preferences are stored on your device. The app and its widgets share the data needed to show your selected vocabulary through an on-device App Group. No cloud sync is included.

Audio and connections

The current app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and does not send learning activity to a SnapHSK server. Pronunciation uses iOS speech synthesis and voices installed on your device. Voice downloads are managed by Apple's system settings.

Optional feedback

If you choose to email feedback, your email app opens a draft. Nothing is sent until you send it. Feedback may include a vocabulary entry ID, the content you report, the app version, your message, and sender information included by your email provider. Email is handled by your email provider and the support mailbox provider. Please do not send sensitive information.

Your choices

You can reset learning progress in Settings. Removing the app removes its local data subject to your device's backup and restore settings. To ask about feedback you sent or request its deletion, email alice.snaphsk@gmail.com.

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change.

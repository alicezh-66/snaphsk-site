About the app

SnapHSK practices HSK 2.0 vocabulary for levels 1–6. It is not a complete exam course and does not guarantee an examination result. HSK 3.0 is not included in v1.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for information about local learning data and optional feedback email.

Vocabulary sources

SnapHSK's HSK 2.0 vocabulary catalog adapts lexical material from CC-CEDICT (published by MDBG; original CEDICT by Paul Andrew Denisowski). The adaptation includes definitions, short meanings, examples and usage notes. This adapted vocabulary content is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. The app code and other materials have separate terms.
